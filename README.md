<h1>📊IMDb Movies Dashboard</h1>
<p>This interactive IMDb Movies Dashboard provides a visual summary of trends, ratings, and revenue insights derived from a dataset of popular movies. The dashboard was built to explore and analyze movie industry data across various dimensions such as time, genre, popularity, and financial performance.</p>

<h2>Dashboard Highlights</h2>
<h3>Key Metrics:</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Average Gross Revenue</strong>: $44,730,770.14</li>
  <li><strong>Average Rating</strong>: 7.95</li>
  <li><strong>Average Meta Score</strong>: 78.00</li>
</ul>
<h3>Top Voted Movies</h3>
<p>A horizontal bar chart displays the most voted movies in the dataset, including classics like The Shawshank Redemption, The Dark Knight, Pulp Fiction, and The Godfather. These movies have the highest number of IMDb votes, indicating strong audience engagement.</p>
<h3> Revenue & Production Trends Over Time</h3>
<ul>
  <li><p><strong>Total Revenue per Year</strong>: A time series line chart reveals how movie revenue has evolved from 1930 to 2020. Revenue shows a significant upward trend starting around the late 1980s, peaking in the early 2000s.</p></li>
  <li><p><strong>Total Movies by Release Year</strong>: Another time series line chart shows the number of movies released per year, with a steady increase over time and a notable spike in production from the 1990s onward.</p></li>
</ul>
<h3>Revenue & Ratings by Genre</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Total Revenue per Genre:</strong> Box plot comparing genres by revenue. Action and Adventure films dominate financially.</li>
      <li><strong>Total Ratings per Genre:</strong> IMDb ratings vary by genre; Biography, Drama, and Crime tend to rate highest.</li>
    </ul>
    <h2>Key Questions This Dashboard Answers</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Which genres generate the highest total revenue?</li>
      <li>What is the trend in total movie releases and revenue over the decades?</li>
      <li>How do IMDb ratings vary across different genres?</li>
      <li>Which movies received the most audience votes?</li>
      <li>How have movie ratings and earnings evolved over time?</li>
      <li>Are high-rated movies always high earners?</li>
      <li>What genre consistently maintains high critical acclaim?</li>
    </ul>
    <h2>Tools & Technologies Used</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Data Visualization:</strong> Tableau</li>
      <li><strong>Data Cleaning & Processing:</strong> Python (Pandas, NumPy)</li>
      <li><strong>Data Source:</strong> IMDb dataset (Kaggle)</li>
    </ul>
    <h2>Insights</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Action and Adventure</strong> genres lead in total revenue, but <strong>Drama and Biography</strong> score higher in ratings.</li>
      <li>The <strong>2000s</strong> saw a boom in both revenue and the number of movie releases.</li>
      <li>Top voted movies are not always the top earners but remain audience favorites with high acclaim.</li>
    </ul>
